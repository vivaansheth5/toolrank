About Us

Built for strength. Designed for elegance.

LEMRA Aluminium & Glass, a unit of LEMRA Construction Pvt. Ltd., is a Ghaziabad-based manufacturer of UPVC doors and windows and a specialist in aluminium and glass work.

From slim-profile sliding doors and tilt & turn windows to frameless glass partitions, railings and facades, we handle every project end to end: measurement, fabrication and installation.
